Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2025

1. Introduction

Split Expense (“the Extension”) is a Chrome extension designed to help users manage and split expenses with friends using Google Sheets as a backend. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Extension.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Info: Basic profile and email for authentication.
  • Expense Data: Expense details stored in your Google Sheets.
  • Group Data: Groups and members you create or join.
  • Usage Data: Limited technical logs stored locally for functionality.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate your account, manage groups, calculate balances, and synchronize expenses with Google Sheets. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally in Chrome and in your Google Sheets/Drive account. We do not operate external servers to store your information.

5. Permissions

The Extension requires permissions such as storage, identity, Google Sheets & Drive API access.

6. Data Security

We use Google OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication. Data is kept under your account ownership.

7. Your Choices

You may revoke permissions from your Google Account or uninstall the extension anytime.

8. Children’s Privacy

Not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be reflected on this page.
